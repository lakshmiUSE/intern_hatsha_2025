Leo lived in a village where the sky was a vast, blue canvas, and clouds were his favorite toys. Every morning, he’d lie in the meadow, watching shapes drift by – dragons, castles, giant fluffy sheep. One day, he decided to catch one. He ran, leaping and stretching, but the cloud always stayed just out of reach. He grew frustrated, kicking the grass. Then, an old woman tending flowers smiled. "Why chase them, little one?" she asked. "Just be with them." Leo stopped running. He sat, watched the clouds, and imagined them whispering secrets. Suddenly, he realized he wasn't chasing them anymore; he was exploring them, one imaginative adventure at a time, finding joy not in catching, but in simply seeing.
